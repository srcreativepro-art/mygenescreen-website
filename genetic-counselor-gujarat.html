Gujarat Population Genetics

Why Gujarat Needs
Genetic Counseling

Gujarat's unique demographic and community genetics create specific, elevated risks that go unaddressed when genetic testing is done without a qualified counselor to interpret the results.

8–10%

Thalassemia Carrier Rate

Gujarat has one of India's highest thalassemia carrier rates. In some communities, nearly 1 in 10 individuals carries a beta-thalassemia gene — making pre-marital and pre-pregnancy screening critical.

Founder
Variants

Community Genetics

Large Jain, Patel, and other endogamous communities in Gujarat carry distinct founder genetic variants. Consanguineous marriages further increase the risk of recessive genetic conditions appearing in children.

Rising

Hereditary Cancer Risk

BRCA1 and BRCA2 mutations associated with hereditary breast and ovarian cancer are increasingly identified in Gujarati families. Genetic counseling before and after testing is essential for informed decision-making.

Gap in
Counseling

WES/WGS Without Guidance

Genomic testing (WES/WGS) is now widely available in Gujarat, but there are very few BGCI certified counselors to interpret variant reports and guide families — leaving patients without the support they need.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from patients in Gujarat about genetic counseling, testing, and how to book an appointment.

Is there a BGCI certified genetic counselor in Gujarat?
Yes — Lakshita Thakore (BGC 2022-178) is one of the very few BGCI Board Certified Genetic Counselors practicing in Gujarat. She sees patients in person at her Memnagar, Ahmedabad clinic and offers online consultations for patients anywhere in Gujarat. BGCI certification is the national standard for genetic counselors in India, and certified practitioners remain rare across the country.
Can I see a genetic counselor online if I live in Surat, Vadodara, or Rajkot?
Absolutely. Online genetic counseling via video call (Google Meet or WhatsApp Video) is available Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 8 PM, for patients from any city in Gujarat and across India. The quality of counseling is the same as an in-person visit — you will receive the same depth of discussion, report interpretation, and family history assessment as if you were sitting in the clinic.
Is thalassemia carrier testing available in Gujarat?
Thalassemia carrier blood testing (HbA2 measurement via HPLC) is widely available in Gujarat — most diagnostic labs offer it for ₹500–1,500. What most people lack is a qualified genetic counselor to explain the results and discuss options, especially when both partners are carriers. When both partners carry a thalassemia gene, there is a 25% chance with each pregnancy that the child will have thalassemia major. That is where MyGeneScreen Clinic can help — we explain your results, outline your options, and support you through this decision.

Being able to express complex medical history and emotional concerns in your first language makes counseling more effective and less stressful.
Do I need a doctor's referral for genetic counseling in Gujarat?
No referral is needed. You can book directly via WhatsApp (+91 97172 10124) or the contact form on this page. A referral from your physician or gynecologist is helpful when they have already ordered tests, but it is never a requirement.
