Online Services

What We Counsel Online

All these specialties are available as tele-counseling sessions — same standard of care, wherever you are in India.

Prenatal Genetic Counseling

NIPT results, anomaly scan findings, chromosomal conditions, and family history risk — discussed clearly before or during pregnancy.

BRCA & Hereditary Cancer Risk

Assessment of BRCA1/2, Lynch syndrome, and other hereditary cancer syndromes. Understand your risk and next steps before any decisions.

WES / WGS Report Interpretation

Plain-language explanation of Whole Exome and Whole Genome Sequencing reports — variants of uncertain significance, pathogenic variants, and clinical implications.

Rare & Undiagnosed Diseases

Support for families navigating a rare or as-yet undiagnosed genetic condition — diagnosis pathways, test selection, and family planning.

Genetic Counseling for Autism / ASD

Genetic evaluation options, copy number variant testing, and family recurrence risk counseling for autism spectrum and neurodevelopmental conditions.

Carrier Screening & Preconception

Before you try to conceive: understand your carrier status for conditions like thalassemia, SMA, cystic fibrosis, and plan with confidence.
