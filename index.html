About Lakshita Thakore

We Offer Expert Genetic Counseling

Lakshita Thakore is a Consultant Geneticist and Board Certified Genetic Counselor (BGCI Reg. BGC 2022-178) with 7+ years of experience in clinical genetics, genomic diagnostics, and precision medicine. Currently pursuing a PhD, she holds an MSc in Biotechnology, a PGD in Genetic Counselling, and a Diploma in Genome Analysis. She serves as Head of Genomics at Navigene Genetic Science Pvt. Ltd. and is associated with HCG Cancer Hospital, Wings Nova IVF, Genexplore, and Pairend NGS Cloud.
