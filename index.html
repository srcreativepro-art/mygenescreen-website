Genetic Counseling — Common Questions

Everything you need to know about genetic counseling in Ahmedabad and across India.

Genetic counseling is a healthcare service that helps individuals and families understand how genetic conditions may affect their health. You may benefit from genetic counseling if you are pregnant or planning to conceive, have a family history of a genetic disorder, are at risk for hereditary cancer (e.g. BRCA mutations, Lynch syndrome), have a child with a genetic condition, or have an unexplained medical condition with a possible genetic cause. Both in-person (Ahmedabad) and online consultations are available across India.

A genetic counseling session at MyGeneScreen Clinic costs ₹1,500 per session (40–45 minutes). Both in-person consultations at our Memnagar, Ahmedabad clinic and online/tele-counseling sessions for patients across India are available at the same fee. No hidden charges.
