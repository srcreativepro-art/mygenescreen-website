Is This Right For You?

Who Should Consider Hereditary Cancer Counseling?

Genetic counseling is not just for people who already have cancer — it is especially valuable before cancer develops, when you have the most options. Consider a session if any of the following apply.

Ask Lakshita Directly
A close relative (parent, sibling, child) had breast, ovarian, colorectal, or uterine cancer before age 50
Multiple family members on the same side of your family had cancer
A relative had cancer in both organs — bilateral breast cancer, or cancer in both kidneys
A male relative had breast cancer — a rare but strong indicator of a BRCA gene variant
You have already had cancer and want to understand your risk of a second cancer
A family member has tested positive for a BRCA or Lynch Syndrome gene variant
You are of Ashkenazi Jewish descent — this population has a significantly higher BRCA carrier rate
Your oncologist or gynecologist has recommended genetic testing but you don't know which test to choose

You do not need a referral letter to book. Self-referrals are welcome. If you are unsure whether counseling is right for you, WhatsApp Lakshita at +91 97172 10124 — she'll respond the same day.

What We Assess

Hereditary Cancer Syndromes & Gene Testing

Lakshita Thakore counsels on the full spectrum of hereditary cancer syndromes — from common BRCA-related conditions to rarer inherited cancer predispositions.

BRCA1 & BRCA2 Gene Testing

The most common hereditary breast and ovarian cancer genes. BRCA1 carriers face up to 72% lifetime breast cancer risk and 44% ovarian cancer risk. BRCA2 carriers have elevated risk for breast, ovarian, pancreatic, and prostate cancer.

BRCA1BRCA2

Lynch Syndrome / HNPCC

The most common hereditary colorectal cancer syndrome. Also significantly increases risk of uterine, ovarian, gastric, and urinary tract cancers. Caused by variants in mismatch repair genes — testing can protect the whole family.

MLH1MSH2MSH6PMS2EPCAM

Familial Adenomatous Polyposis (FAP)

Caused by variants in the APC gene, FAP leads to hundreds of colorectal polyps that progress to cancer if untreated. Early identification allows timely surveillance and preventive surgery to protect high-risk family members.

APCMUTYH

Hereditary Ovarian Cancer

Not all hereditary ovarian cancer is caused by BRCA. Several other genes confer significant ovarian cancer risk. Genetic counseling identifies the precise gene involved, which determines the most appropriate surveillance and prevention strategy.

RAD51CRAD51DBRIP1

Other Hereditary Cancer Genes

Beyond BRCA, dozens of genes confer elevated cancer risk. Lakshita counsels across the full panel spectrum — moderate-risk genes that are frequently missed on single-gene tests but still require surveillance and management changes.

PALB2CHEK2ATMCDH1STK11VHL

Cancer Risk Reduction Options

A positive genetic result is not a death sentence — it is an opportunity. Lakshita helps you understand all risk-reduction pathways available to you, including enhanced surveillance, chemoprevention, prophylactic surgery, and PGT-M for IVF planning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about hereditary cancer genetic counseling with Lakshita Thakore in Ahmedabad.

Hereditary cancer genetic counseling is a medical consultation that helps you understand whether cancer in your family has a genetic cause. A genetic counselor reviews your full family history, assesses your risk using validated clinical models, recommends appropriate genetic testing, and helps you understand your results and management options. It is not the same as seeing an oncologist — a genetic counselor focuses specifically on inherited cancer syndromes and risk communication.

Several Indian labs — including Strand Life Sciences, Medgenome, Neuberg Diagnostics, and Thyrocare's genomics arm — offer hereditary cancer gene panels covering BRCA1/2, Lynch syndrome genes (MLH1, MSH2, MSH6, PMS2, EPCAM), PALB2, CHEK2, ATM, CDH1, STK11, and others. Panel costs typically range from ₹10,000–₹35,000 depending on the scope. A genetic counselor helps you choose the right test for your specific family history and ensures you're not under- or over-tested.

Standard cancer screening tests — such as a mammogram or colonoscopy — detect cancer early once it has formed, but they do not tell you your underlying genetic risk. This information is far more powerful — it helps you and your doctors make proactive decisions about surveillance and prevention before cancer develops, not just detect it after the fact.
